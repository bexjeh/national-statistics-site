Methods & sources

Every figure on this site is compiled by the National Statistics Office of Caldoria from a named survey or register, checked before publication, and revised openly when better data arrive.

How figures are checked

1. Collection: trained interviewers and audited business returns; missing answers are followed up, not guessed.

2. Validation: every series is checked against the previous period, the same period last year, and independent registers. A move larger than 3 standard deviations is investigated before publication.

3. Seasonal adjustment: GDP, unemployment and prices are published both raw and seasonally adjusted; this site shows the adjusted series.

4. Revision: quarterly figures are revised twice as late data arrive. Revisions are published with the old and new values side by side.

Definitions

Unemployment rate: unemployed people as a share of the labour force (employed + unemployed), ages 15–74.

GDP: the value of all goods and services produced in Caldoria, in current prices unless “real” is stated.

CPI inflation: the change in the consumer prices index over 12 months.

Median wage: the wage at the middle of the distribution — half of full-time workers earn more, half earn less.

Caldor (CLD): Caldoria's currency. 1 CLD = 100 senti.
